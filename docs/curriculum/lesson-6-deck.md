# Ask it well

<p class="lede">Lesson 6: asking a good question is a skill, and you can learn it.</p>

<p class="muted">Check the Source · 45 minutes</p>

## If you walked up to me and said "space"...

<div class="quote">

I'd ask what you want to know about space. The assistant usually doesn't ask. It **guesses**.

Today you learn to stop making it guess.

</div>

## Weak and strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| tell me about space | How do astronauts sleep on the International Space Station? |
| climate | How does warming ocean water hurt coral reefs? |
| help with my report | I'm writing a report on the Dust Bowl. What caused it, and how did it change farming? |
| who is best president | Which U.S. presidents do historians rank highest, and why do they disagree? |

## The four parts

<div class="steps">
<div class="card-panel"><strong>Subject</strong><p>Exactly what it's about. "Gray wolves", not
"animals".</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><strong>Specific question</strong><p>What you want to know. "What do they
eat in winter?"</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><strong>Purpose</strong><p>Why you're asking. "For a report."</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><strong>Limits</strong><p>What you want back. "Three causes."</p></div>
</div>

<p class="muted">You don't need all four, but you need more than one.</p>

## Habits of good askers

- Use the words **a book would use**. "Coral bleaching" finds more than "why reefs look dead".
- Ask **one thing at a time**, then follow up.
- If the first answer misses, **change the prompt**. A prompt is a draft.
- **Leave out personal details**, yours and anyone else's.

---

## Your turn: the prompt ladder

<div class="steps">
<div class="card-panel"><strong>One word</strong><p>"Wolves"</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><strong>A specific question</strong><p>What do you actually want to
know?</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><strong>Add a purpose or limits</strong><p>Why, and what do you want
back?</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><strong>Go to the shelf</strong><p>Check one claim against your best
book.</p></div>
</div>

<p class="muted">Topics: gray wolves, volcanoes, the Civil War, the Dust Bowl, coral reefs, the
Great Lakes, the moon landing.</p>

## What did you find?

- Which rung changed the **most**?
- Did a stronger prompt ever make things **worse**?
- Was your best answer also **true**?

<div class="box">

Very narrow questions can find fewer books. Go one step **broader for books**, and **narrower
for the answer**.

</div>

---

## Strong doesn't mean true

<div class="quote">

A strong prompt gets you a better answer, not a guaranteed true one. **Keep checking.**

</div>
