## The trail

## How to read a call number

- **Nonfiction** has a number. Find the hundreds, then the exact number, then the letters.
- **FIC** is fiction, by the author's last name.
- **B** is biography, by the name of the person it's about.
- **E** is picture books.

## Take it with you

- Every answer has **two parts**: the overview, and Books in our library.
- A **Citation** gives a book's title and call number. A **Record** describes the book.
- A Citation proves the book is **real** and that we own it. It doesn't prove the overview is
  right.
- The one line about why a book was picked is the model talking. Check it against the book.
