## Take it with you

- **Stop making it guess.** Say what you actually want to know.
- **Specific beats long.** Extra words aren't the point.
- **A prompt is a draft.** Change it when it isn't working.
- **Strong doesn't mean true.** A sharper question can still get an invented name or date.
- A book named in the overview may not be in our library. Only **Books in our library** are
  real Records.

## Check yourself

   Rome build aqueducts?"
