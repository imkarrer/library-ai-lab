## What's in a Record

A **Record** describes a book: **title**, **author**, **subjects**, **summary**, and **call
number**.

<div class="box">

A Record is **not** the book. It also doesn't say whether the book is on the shelf right now.

</div>

The one line about why a book was picked is the model talking, so check that too.

---

## Your turn: Citation relay

Ask three questions from the list. For each answer:

- The **typer** asks.
- The **reader** reads the overview aloud.
- The **checker** opens the Citations and copies title and call number.
- The **runner** goes to the shelf.
- The **recorder** fills in the Citation trail on the back of your handout.

<p class="muted">Book checked out? Mark it. That still counts: the Record is real.</p>

## What did you find?

- Did every book in Books in our library turn out to be **real**?
- Did the books seem to be about **what the overview said**?

<div class="box">

A Citation proves the book is real and that we own it. It doesn't prove the overview is right.

</div>
