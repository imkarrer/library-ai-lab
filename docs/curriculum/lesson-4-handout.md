## When a message worries the adults

The assistant checks every message for signs that someone could be in danger. If it finds one,
it shows a message written for you and lets the librarian know, who may bring in the counselor.
That's not to get anyone in trouble. It's because the adults here would rather know.

## The honest parts

- An adult can find out who had a Card. They can ask the teacher.
- An adult may check in even if the answer looked normal.
- The assistant can miss things. It's not a counselor. **Talking to a person is always the
  better way.**

## Take it with you

- **No name** is in the assistant. The librarian sees questions, and an adult can find out who
