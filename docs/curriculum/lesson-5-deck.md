# Using AI on assignments

<p class="lede">Lesson 5: know what's allowed, and say what you did.</p>

## When you quote a book, why do you cite it?

- **Credit** to the person who wrote it
- So others **can check** it
- **Honesty** about what's yours

<div class="box">

The same reasons apply to AI, plus one more: AI can be wrong, so your reader needs to know
**which parts to double-check**.

</div>

<!--

do, not as a confession.

## Three AI Use Tiers

| Tier | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Human-Only** | No AI tools. Your own thinking, your own words. | An in-class quiz |
| **AI-Assist** | AI for a step your teacher names. Check it and disclose it. | Finding books for a research project |
| **AI-Optional** | AI more freely. Still disclose and check. You're responsible for everything you turn in. | A practice activity on AI answers |

<div class="box">

Your teacher sets the tier **for each assignment**. When in doubt, **ask**.

</div>

<!--

## The disclosure line

<div class="quote">

**AI use:** I used ______ (tool) to ______ (what for) on ______ (date). I kept ______ and changed
______. I checked it by ______.

</div>

<div class="cards">
<div class="card-panel"><h3>Tool, purpose</h3><p>What did you use, and for what?</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><h3>Kept, changed</h3><p>What did you do with it?</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><h3>How checked</h3><p>Be specific.</p></div>
</div>

<!--
Adapted from DPI's copy-ready disclosure box. High school: add your school's citation style.
-->

## An example

<div class="quote">

AI use: I used the Library Assistant to find books about volcanoes on March 3. I kept two of the
books it suggested and skipped one that was about earthquakes. I checked it by finding both books
on the shelf and reading their tables of contents.

</div>

<div class="cards two">
<div class="card-panel"><h3>Too vague</h3><p>"I looked it up."</p></div>
<div class="card-panel"><h3>Specific</h3><p>"I found it on the shelf at 551.21."</p></div>
</div>

<p class="muted">Human-Only assignment? Your line is: "AI use: none."</p>

## Your turn: write your line

Write a disclosure line for your **Lesson 3 audit**, as if it were an AI-Assist assignment.

Then swap with a partner. Does it say:

- what **tool**?
- what **for**?
- what was **kept or changed**?
- **how** it was checked?

<!--
12 minutes. "How checked" is the part most often left vague. The line itself is the formative
check.

## One last question

<div class="quote">

If the assistant sounds really sure, is the answer right?

</div>

Answer again, now with **a reason** and **a way to check**.
