## Take it with you

- A language model **predicts likely next words**. It doesn't look facts up on its own.
- It **sounds just as sure** when it's guessing as when it's right.
- The **overview** is the model talking. **Books in our library** are real books we own.
- The overview is often a good start, and it can be wrong about anything. Checking is how you
  use it well, not a sign that it's broken.
- This assistant runs on a computer in our building.
