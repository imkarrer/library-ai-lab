# Who sees what I type?

<p class="lede">Lesson 4: your Card, your questions, and who is here to help.</p>

<p class="muted">Check the Source · 45 minutes</p>

## The Card, up close

- The QR code holds a **secret** that lets the Card start a chat.
- The assistant keeps a **scrambled** copy of that secret, the Card number, the class, your
  teacher's name, and when the Card stops working.
- **It never gets your name.**
- Your teacher keeps a list of who had which Card number. That list never goes to the assistant.

<p class="muted">A Card stops working when the time runs out, by the end of the school day at the
latest, or earlier if an adult turns it off.</p>

## Who can see what

| The assistant | The librarian | Your teacher's list |
|---|---|---|
| Your questions and its answers | Questions as they're asked, labeled "Period 3, Card 14" | Which student had Card 14 |
| Card number, class, and your teacher's name | The answers | Nothing you typed |
| **Not your name** | **Not your name**, unless they ask your teacher | |

<p class="muted">"The librarian" includes any staff member the librarian asks to cover. A school
counselor sees a question only if the librarian asks them to help with it.</p>

## When a message worries the adults

<div class="box">

Sometimes people type things to a computer they wouldn't say out loud. The assistant checks every
message for signs that someone could be in danger. If it finds one, it shows a message written for
what you might be going through, and it **lets the librarian know**.

</div>

That's not to get anyone in trouble. It's because **the adults here would rather know**.

## The honest parts

- **An adult can find out who had the Card.** They can ask the teacher. That happens between
  adults, not inside the assistant.
- **An adult may check in even if the answer looked normal.** Some concerns just let an adult know.
- **The assistant can miss things.** It's not a counselor.

<div class="box">

If something is wrong, **talking to a person** is always the better way.

</div>

## Your turn: who can see it?

<p class="muted">For each one: who can see this, and what happens next?</p>

- A student asks for books about sharks.
- A student types their full name and address into a question.
- A student asks for books about grief because their grandpa died.
- A student types "I want to disappear" and nothing else.
- A student asks the assistant to write their whole book report.
- A student shares their Card with a friend in another class.
- It's 4 p.m. What is still saved from this morning's questions?

## Before you go

- **"What happens to what I type"** says all of this, any time. It's linked from the Card sign-in
  and chat pages.
- On your handout, write **one adult here** you could talk to. Nobody will collect it. It's for
  you.
