# Transcripts are treated as pupil records

A transcript filed by Class label and Card number can be linked to a Student by the school
through the Teacher's Roster, so while it exists it is an education record under FERPA
(34 CFR 99.3) and a behavioral record under Wis. Stat. 118.125, with parent inspection rights
(45 days under FERPA, 34 CFR 99.10(b)). Wis. Stat. 43.30 (library records) may apply too: its
text covers any library supported in whole or in part by public funds, not only public
libraries, so the design must satisfy it as well, and whether Identification is allowed under
it is a question for district counsel. We design so that the assistant holds no transcript
tied to a Student and holds even pseudonymous transcripts only briefly (ADR-0002, ADR-0003).
Treating transcripts as pupil records also matters for the Purge: if they were ordinary public
records, Wis. Stat. 19.21 would require keeping them far longer. COPPA reaches only operators
acting for commercial purposes, so it does not reach a school-run, non-commercial Install,
which is a structural advantage over commercial chatbots for 11-to-13-year-olds; keep it that
way by never adding a commercial operator to the loop. The reasoning and citations are in
`docs/templates/compliance/ferpa-analysis.md`.
